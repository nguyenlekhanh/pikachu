## Is commercial livestreaming allowed?

**Yes, with attribution.** Incompetech's own FAQ answers this directly:

> "Can I use your music... on streaming sites? Yes. Be sure to credit me. ... in live productions?
> Yes. Be sure to credit me."

CC BY 4.0 permits commercial use, public performance and broadcast, including a monetized TikTok
LIVE, provided the credit is visible/available to the audience. There is no per-stream royalty and
no platform restriction in the license itself.

### Required attribution (must be visible on stream)

The licensor requires: the composer's name, the track title(s), a link to the source, and the
license. Any one of these placements satisfies it (per
https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/twitchFAQ.html):

1. A "Music" section on your profile / stream description listing every track used, **or**
2. The credits shown as an overlay or in the stream description, **or**
3. Announced out loud on the stream.

Copy-paste block (use this on TikTok / in your stream description):

```
Music by Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com)
Tracks: Balloon Game, Happy Happy Game Show, Pixelland, Bit Shift, Overworld,
Fluffing a Duck, Monkeys Spinning Monkeys, Carefree, Life of Riley,
Jaunty Gumption, Silly Fun, Spazzmatica Polka, Happy Bee
Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
```

Per-track form (required wording from the FAQ):

```
"<Track Title>" Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com)
Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
```

## Sources that were not used

| Source | Result |
|---|---|
| Pixabay Music (https://pixabay.com/music/) | Automated access blocked (HTTP 403 from its bot protection). Its Pixabay Content License does allow commercial use, but downloads must be done in a browser. Browse manually if you want more tracks; do not bypass the block. |
| YouTube Audio Library | Requires a signed-in Google account; there is no anonymous download API. Also, downloading audio from YouTube videos is prohibited, so this library must be used manually from youtube.com/audiolibrary. |
| Mixkit | Excluded: its free music license forbids use in video games and in TV/radio broadcast, which makes it a poor fit for a game overlay. |
